MITRSETU BILLING - HOW TO INSTALL
=================================

1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All...", then click Extract.
   (Do not run it from inside the zip.)

2. Open the extracted folder and double-click  INSTALL.bat

   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
   click  More info  ->  Run anyway.

3. That's it. The app opens, and a "MITRSETU Billing" icon is added
   to your Desktop and Start Menu. You can delete the extracted folder now.

FIRST TIME USE
- Type your GSTIN once in the GSTIN box at the top (it is remembered).
- When you save the first PDF, choose the folder for your bills
  (for example D:\MITRSETU Bills). Click "Allow" if Chrome/Edge asks.

THE THREE TABS
- GST Invoice   : Tax invoice with GST % per item (CGST+SGST or IGST).
                  Numbers: MST/26-27/0001 ...
- Non-GST Bill  : Bill of Supply, no GST.          Numbers: MS/26-27/0001 ...
- Quotation     : Price quote to send to customers.  Numbers: QT/26-27/0001 ...
                  Saved in the "Quotations" folder. Use "Share PDF" to send it
                  on WhatsApp / email, and "Make Bill from Quote" to turn it
                  into a bill.

SAVE PDF & PRINT
  One click saves the PDF in your Bills folder (named with the customer
  name) and then opens the print window. You do not need to save
  anything from the print window - just print, or press Cancel.

PDFs are saved with the customer name and number:
  <Bills folder>\GST Bills\01-11-2026\SHARMA ELECTRICALS_MST-26-27-0001.pdf
  <Bills folder>\Non-GST Bills\01-11-2026\DEEPAK_MS-26-27-0001.pdf
  <Bills folder>\Quotations\01-11-2026\RAHUL KUMAR_QT-26-27-0001.pdf

OPEN A SAVED BILL
  Click "Open Bill (PDF)" and pick a PDF saved by this app. All details are
  filled in again. Then choose:
  - "Open this bill to correct / reprint" (keeps the same number), or
  - "New GST Invoice / New Non-GST Bill / New Quotation" with the same
    customer and items (gets the next number).
  (PDFs made by older versions of the app cannot be opened this way.)

UPDATING
  Extract the new zip and double-click INSTALL.bat again.
  Your invoice numbers, drafts and settings are kept.

REMOVING
  Double-click UNINSTALL.bat. Your saved PDF bills are not deleted.

Works on Windows 10 / 11 with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
